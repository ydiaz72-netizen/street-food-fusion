Who we are

Two kitchens.
One table.

Wepa is the place in Erfurt where cultures meet, strangers become neighbours, and every meal is an invitation to discover another corner of the world. Born from a Puerto Rican family and a Pakistani kitchen — two households that believed food is how you show love.

🇵🇷

Puerto Rico

Where food is how you show love. Empanadas, mofongo, pernil — Caribbean soul that carries five centuries of history in every bite.

🇵🇰

Pakistan

Generations of spice, warmth, and home cooking. Samosa, pakora, biryani — made from scratch, the way it was always meant to be.

🇵🇷 San Juan + 🇵🇰 Karachi = 🌍 Erfurt
